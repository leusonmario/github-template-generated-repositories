# Consumer survey

The study includes an online, voluntary survey of practitioners associated with template-generated repositories. The questionnaire concerns template adoption, changes after generation, awareness and handling of quality/security concerns, and reactions to consumer-oriented recommendations.

## Recruitment and response

- 4,538 invitations attempted; 4,215 delivered.
- The questionnaire was open for **two weeks**.
- 38 responses were received (38/4,215 = **0.90%** response rate).
- 32 responses met the study's self-reported template-consumer eligibility criterion and were retained for analysis.

These responses complement the observational evidence; do not generalize their distributions to all template consumers.

## Privacy and ethics

Institutional ethics approval was obtained before recruitment. Never publish raw contact lists, participant emails, IP addresses, open-ended identifiable responses, or data that would allow individual respondents to be reconstructed. Publish only appropriately de-identified responses and aggregate tables, subject to the participant information and ethics approval conditions.

The manuscript uses selected **closed-ended** responses to complement RQ1–RQ3 and the consumer guidelines. An anonymized questionnaire, codebook, and aggregate response data should be included if the approval and consent permit.
